What the loss exercise does

The exercise advances the local clock by a month. Your existing approval permits a 100 copied PVT no-earnings contribution and the next 100 copied PVT monthly burn. It then simulates a 100 PVT theft and restores it to a public test destination: 60 PVT by reversal and 40 PVT from the pool. The local simulator impersonates accounts for the loss and recovery transactions. Exchange evidence and recovery material are synthetic. Never send real funds to the test destination.
