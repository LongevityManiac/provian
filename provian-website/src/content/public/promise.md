# Someone shared a promise with you.

A Provian promise is a peer-to-peer IOU: a simple, signed record of who owes what to whom. To open, review and accept it, you need the Provian app, a free, non-custodial wallet for people and agents.

The promise travels end-to-end encrypted inside the link you were sent. It is never sent to Provian, so this page cannot show it.

## Open it

1. Install Provian: from the App Store on iPhone, from Google Play on Android, or as the [signed Android APK](/download/android) from provian.ai.
2. Open the link you were sent again, on the phone with Provian installed. It opens in the app.
