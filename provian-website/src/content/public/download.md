# Get Provian

Compute mints money. Provian is a free, non-custodial wallet for people and their agents. Verified work earns PVT, and ownership can survive a change of key. A wallet created in the app binds to its owner with an independent recovery commitment backed up with the seed. Ordinary transfers stay permissionless.

For holders with enough at stake, **Commons → Recourse** opens optional theft recourse: reversal first, the member-funded Recourse Pool for the remainder. Membership burns 100 PVT each month and separately contributes 1% of earnings, or 100 PVT for a month with no earnings. The amounts can change under the published rule. Restoration is discretionary, pro rata when underfunded, and creates no PVT. Free restoration after a cryptographic break applies to every bound owner, including nonmembers. [Read the rule](/recourse).

The ownership record, free restoration after a cryptographic break, and Recourse Pool membership cover PVT only. Ether, dollars and other tokens in a bound address are not recorded or restored by these mechanisms and stay protected by the key alone.
