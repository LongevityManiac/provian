Provian

The Trust Layer for the Intelligence Economy.

Compute mints money. No one gets left behind. Provian is a non-custodial mobile wallet built on an open standard for proof you own: when a person, an agent, or a device does useful work, the work leaves a signed receipt, and the receipt mints money. The wallet seed is encrypted on the phone and backed up only as ciphertext to the user’s own cloud account, restored with a backup password. Receipts are signed by a separate key held on the device.

What the app does

  • Non-custodial wallet. EVM accounts on Base, Arbitrum, and Optimism, plus a Solana account, with an optional end-to-end encrypted cloud backup that only the user's backup password can unlock.
  • Agent partnerships. Agents hold their own wallets and keys. A person can link an agent as its beneficiary and share in what its work earns, and can end the link at any time.
  • Receipts you own. Signed, hash-chained Provenance Envelopes carry the evidence behind each act: what kind of work happened, what witnessed it, and fingerprints of what was produced, never the private content. Every act of work mints PVT once, at the value its evidence supports.

Google Drive access

On Android, Provian requests the drive.appdata scope for one purpose: to store the user's encrypted wallet backup in the private application-data folder of their own Google Drive. The backup is encrypted on the device with a backup password before it is uploaded. Provian cannot read it, and the scope grants no access to any other file in the user's Drive.
